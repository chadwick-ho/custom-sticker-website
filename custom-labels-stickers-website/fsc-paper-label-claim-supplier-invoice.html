FSC Paper Label Requests: Check the Product Claim and the Invoice

If a supplement brand asks for FSC paper labels, specify that requirement before the material is quoted. Then check the supplier's current certificate scope and how the actual product will be identified with an FSC claim on the sales documents. A paper mill's certificate or a logo pasted into the artwork does not, by itself, establish the status of your finished-label purchase.

This is a paper-sourcing decision. It does not approve adhesive performance, printed copy, food-contact use or recyclability of the finished bottle. Keep those requirements in the same project brief but give each its own evidence.

Identify which part of the order the request concerns

A label order can contain paper face material, a paper release liner and a shipping carton. Do not leave the phrase “FSC material” to stand for all three. Ask the supplier to identify the proposed certified product and claim, including which parts of the supplied goods are covered.

For a proposed film label with a paper liner, a statement about the liner should not be promoted as proof that the film face is certified paper. Likewise, a claim for the shipping carton does not automatically apply to the labels inside it. Keep the named component and the finished product description visible.

FSC UK's purchasing guidance recommends using a certified supplier, checking the certificate and product category, specifying the requirement when ordering, and checking the product claim and supplier certificate code on the invoice or delivery note. For a printed-label job, ask the supplier to confirm the proposed arrangement within that scope.

Verify the supplier record before relying on the claim

Use the official FSC certificate-search entry point to reach the current search service or dashboard. Search using the supplier's actual certificate or licence code and compare the organization name, certificate status and relevant scope. Save the date and record reference you checked; a screenshot of an old certificate is only historical evidence.

If the sales entity and the manufacturing entity differ, ask how the quoted supply arrangement connects to the certificate and claim. A group relationship is useful context, not a replacement for identifying who will make the sales claim on this order. Give unresolved scope questions to the supplier and its certification body or FSC contact rather than guessing from similar names.

Certificate codes and artwork permissions do different work

FSC's claims and code guidance distinguishes the certificate code used in sales-document claims from the trademark licence code used with FSC labels and promotional use. The existence of one code does not authorize a buyer to compose its own on-product FSC label.

Ask the authorized supplier to provide the appropriate proposed FSC artwork and approval route for the product, where that is part of the agreed order. Keep the approved graphic with the actual artwork revision. Do not copy a symbol from another package, replace its code or add an unapproved text claim as a shortcut.

Use a worked mismatch to test the quote

Imagine a fictional quote for paper bottle labels. The supplier sends a certificate for a paper producer, while its own offer merely says “paper label.” The buyer still lacks a resolved finished-product claim and sales-document arrangement. Return the question before approving a certified-product marketing statement.

A useful reply would identify the proposed product and supplier arrangement, the claim offered and how the sales documents will show it. If the requested route is unavailable, the buyer can compare another confirmed supply option or remove the claim from this purchase. Printing the logo first is not a solution.

When comparing offers, hold the other requirements steady: label dimensions, print, finish, adhesive use, quantities and delivered format. Paper sourcing does not justify silently substituting a construction that has not been approved on the bottle. The paper versus film comparison covers that separate material choice.

Check the actual sale, then control reorders

At the sales-document stage, confirm that the relevant product line is identified with the agreed FSC claim and supplier certificate code. An FSC-certified company can also sell products outside a particular claim. Its general certificate should not make every line of an invoice automatically certified.

Keep that document with the accepted specification and approved artwork. If the supplier, paper source, claim or production arrangement changes on a repeat order, reopen the affected sourcing check. The substitution guide helps connect a changed material reference with the rest of the label approval.

For supplement-label projects, prepare an RP inquiry stating which paper product and sourcing claim you require, the bottle, quantities and destination. Ask whether an appropriately documented supply option is available. RP's published invitation to discuss paper-source documents is not a standing FSC certification claim.
