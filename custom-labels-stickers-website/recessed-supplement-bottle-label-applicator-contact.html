Labels for Recessed Supplement Bottles: Check Applicator Contact

A label can fit inside a recessed bottle panel on a drawing and still fail to make full contact on the line. Confirm both where the label fits and where the applicator can press it against the panel floor. If the outline crosses the raised rim, request a revised shape. If it fits but a roller or pad rides on the rim, have the packer check the contact method before changing label stock.

Put the panel floor and its shoulders on the drawing

Use the supplier's container drawing and physical production bottles. Mark the usable floor, the rim, the transition radius and any embossing or raised feature. For a nonrectangular recess, check the corners where the proposed outline comes closest to the transition. Give prepress a view of the panel from the front and a cross-section showing depth and shoulder geometry. The available floor and the intended label outline should both have identifiable dimensions. The bottle-label size guide covers the initial layout.

The applicator maker's panel design guide describes how a recessed panel can block access for wipe-down. A smaller outline may clear the radius while leaving the center out of reach for the same contact device. Ask the packer to show where its device makes contact on the bottle in the condition that actually goes through the line.

Compare fit and machine contact on separate samples

Have the packer place a final-size sample within the floor by hand for an initial fit reference. Confirm that the outline avoids the shoulder and that the applied piece meets the visual and information-panel requirements. Then compare a finished roll of the proposed construction on the intended applicator. Look for unpressed areas, bubbles or edge lift. Mark where each begins relative to the rim and the application direction. Observe again after the agreed interval so the review can catch a delayed change.

Use representative bottles when there is known panel variation. If a sample differs because the bottle flexes during contact, capture the fill state and support. The wiper contact guide can help locate when the first mark appears. Keep any line setup changes with the packer.

Quote the outline with the contact evidence

Prepare an RP Labels inquiry with the panel drawing and depth, annotated bottle photos, the proposed artwork and quantities per design, and the packer's contact observations. Ask which shape and finished roll can be quoted for the usable floor. If the line trial is still open, carry that status on the proof and purchase approval.
