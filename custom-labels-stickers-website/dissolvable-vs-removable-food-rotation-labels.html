Dissolvable vs. Removable Food Rotation Labels

A central kitchen sends reusable prep containers to locations with different dish-room routines. At one site, staff remove the old date label at the return station. At another, containers with labels still attached go into the wash. Consider removable stock where someone can reliably peel it after the last date check. Consider dissolvable stock where the approved wash process handles label removal and the date stays readable until the container reaches that step. If the two routines call for different stocks, specify them by site or agree on one operating procedure before buying.

Start at the last time staff need the date

Follow a container from prep to storage and return. Ask the team to identify the last step that needs the old date and the person responsible for reading it. For an illustrative route, staff may check the prep date at the return station, peel the label, and place the tub in a wash rack. For a wash-removal route, the label stays attached through that same last read. This comparison covers internal rotation marks on the exterior of reusable containers.

Use the actual tub or lid model, the intended label placement, and the pen or print method staff use. Match the fields and use clearly identified test dates on both samples. Record whether staff apply the label to a dry surface or a surface already cold or damp. Run each sample through representative refrigerated storage, handling, condensation and any routine exterior wipe. Ask an operator to read the old date at the agreed handoff and compare it with the recorded starting value. Record missing digits, smearing, loosened edges or a label that detached before the read.

Follow each sample through the assigned removal route

Peel before washing. Have the assigned employee remove the label at the usual station after the date check. Try the approved method on a container at the condition staff normally encounter. Record whether the label comes off in one piece, whether it tears, and whether any adhesive or legible date remains. Run the normal approved wash procedure and inspect the planned label area afterward.

Remove during washing. Run the dissolvable candidate through the site's approved equipment and program. Record the relevant wash conditions with the equipment lead, including the program, loading, and whether spray or water reaches the label area in the normal rack position. Inspect for remaining face material, adhesive and ink or old date marks after the cycle. Ask the equipment lead to check for visible label fragments where their procedure allows inspection. DayMark lists separate removable and dissolvable constructions, and Avery Dennison describes water-disintegrating stock for rotation labels. Ask how the specific label and writing system are intended to work with your process. DayMark adhesive options · Avery Dennison food rotation labels

Put the same container back into the next cycle

After the usual drying and staging step, compare the label area with its starting condition. Check any visible residue and whether someone could read an old date on the container. Apply a fresh label to the intended spot, enter a different test date, and have staff read it after the next normal handling interval. Record edge lift, poor adhesion over a remnant or two dates that could be confused. Use the last read before washing, the removal inspection, and the next application as separate release checks. For example, a stock that washes away as intended still fails a route if wiping erases the date early.

Write the approved stock, pen or print method, read point, removal owner and next-label check into the site's instructions. If the site switches containers, writing tools or wash programs, repeat the relevant checks. A separate RP Labels guide covers trials for an external label in a returnable glass bottle pool.

Describe the kitchen routine in the label request

Send the container and lid models and materials, placement photos, sample date fields, label dimensions, pen or print method, cold-storage and wipe conditions, and the approved removal route by location. Include wash-program details for an in-wash candidate, quantities by design and site, and the sample sets needed for the next-cycle check. Ask which removable or dissolvable constructions can be considered for the project. Discuss a rotation label sample request with RP Labels
