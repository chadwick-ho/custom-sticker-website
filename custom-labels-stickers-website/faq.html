Waterproof and oil-resistant are not the same performance. We can specify labels for cosmetic creams and similar products, but resistance depends on the formula, container, and how the pack is handled. We recommend testing with your product, not assuming a water-resistant label is oil-resistant. See oil-resistant labels.
We can die-cut labels to a custom shape, including a shape that follows your logo outline, when the artwork and size are suitable for converting. Tooling, if required, is confirmed on the written quote. See die cutting.
We supply labels on rolls for automatic applicators and in sheets or individual pieces when that format is a better fit. Roll direction, core size, liner, and spacing are specified to the machine information you provide. The 1–8 unwind chart, core sizes, and liners are specified from your machine information.
