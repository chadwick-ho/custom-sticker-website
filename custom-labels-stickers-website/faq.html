Frequently Asked Questions

Review the main details buyers usually confirm before starting a custom label or sticker project.

The minimum quantity is confirmed on the written quote. It depends on product type, material and process. This page does not publish a standing MOQ.
Ask the quote whether a first order can be smaller than a later run. Availability depends on the construction. This page does not publish a standing minimum.
Ask on the quote whether a digital proof is included before production. A complimentary proof is not a standing offer unless the written quote says so.
Send a logo, text, bottle photo or reference image and ask the quote whether a proof can be prepared from it.
Yes. You can upload your logo or send it through WhatsApp or email at ruishengmao05@gmail.com.
AI, PDF, PSD, SVG, PNG and JPG are commonly accepted. Vector files are preferred when available.
Sample time depends on product type and finish. We will provide an estimated schedule after proof confirmation.
Bulk production time depends on quantity, material and process. We confirm lead time after reviewing order details.
Ask which film or paper the quote proposes when the label needs to resist moisture. A material name on this site is not a certified construction.
Custom shapes depend on the cutting tool. Ask the quote whether a die is required and how it is charged.
Roll supply can be quoted. Core, unwind and face stock are confirmed per order. The About page shows one March 2026 roll example, not every material.
Sheet supply, if needed, is confirmed on the quote. This page does not publish sheet pricing or a minimum.
Send product type, size, quantity, material, finish, artwork and shipping country for a faster quote.
Shipping method, freight and transit are confirmed with the order. This page does not publish a freight rate or transit time. See shipping information.
