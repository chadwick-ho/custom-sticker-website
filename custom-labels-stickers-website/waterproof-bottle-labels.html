Why Waterproof Materials Matter

Labels Built for Condensation, Handling and Shelf Display

Beverage packaging often faces condensation, cold storage, handling and transport. Waterproof BOPP, PET and film materials help keep bottle labels clean-looking and durable in suitable packaging conditions.

Send your bottle material, bottle size, label size, filling and storage conditions, quantity and artwork. RP can recommend a practical material and finish before production.
