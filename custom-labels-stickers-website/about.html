Who We Are

RP is a custom labels and stickers manufacturer serving overseas brands, packaging companies, lab supply sellers, beverage businesses, sticker brands and eCommerce sellers. We focus on practical custom production, clear communication and reliable delivery for B2B label and sticker projects.

Our team helps customers confirm product type, material, size, finish, artwork and proof before production begins.

OEM / ODM Support

We support custom labels and stickers for private label projects, packaging updates and brand product launches.

Small Batch and Bulk Production

Small orders can support testing and launches. Bulk production is available after proof and details are confirmed.

Design Proof Support

Customers can send artwork, logo, reference image or bottle photo. Our team helps prepare a digital proof before production.

Factory Production Capability

Production support includes printing, finishing, cutting, roll labels, sticker sheets, inspection, packing and shipping.

Quality Focus

We pay attention to material suitability, print clarity, cutting accuracy, finish choice and packaging condition.

Efficient Communication

One-to-one WhatsApp support helps overseas buyers confirm details quickly and reduce back-and-forth communication.
