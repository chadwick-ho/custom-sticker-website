RP Labels buyer guides

How to use our purchasing guides

RP Labels publishes these guides as a custom-label supplier. They help buyers prepare specifications and compare approval evidence. They are not independent product certifications or proof that a proposed construction will work on your pack.

Who is responsible for the content?

The named publisher is RP Labels. Our guidance reflects a packaging-supplier viewpoint; a corporate byline does not imply a named independent expert or a completed test.

What counts as evidence?

Manufacturer documents and standards guidance are linked where used and apply within their stated scope. A worked example, conditional scenario or AI packaging illustration is identified as such. It is not a customer order, measured result or certification.

What should you approve separately?

Keep artwork, physical-use evidence and commercial terms distinct. Your responsible team approves the required copy and product claims; agree actual materials, sample differences, quantities and release responsibilities in the project record.

Request a correction or project-specific evidence

Email ruishengmao05@gmail.com with the page URL, the statement to check and the source or application conditions. For a quotation, include the actual pack, artwork revision, quantity per design and the unanswered decision. Prepare the inquiry or review our approval checkpoints.
