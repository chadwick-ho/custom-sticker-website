Should You Preprint SSCC Pallet Labels or Print at Dispatch?

Choose preprinted SSCC-only labels if your warehouse can scan each applied label and bind it to the pallet it just built, and the receiver accepts that label format. Print at pallet build if the label must carry details that become available only when packing is complete. In either workflow, agree who creates the pallet’s identity, who records its contents and who confirms that the outbound shipping record points to the pallet on the dock.

Decide who identifies the physical pallet

A Serial Shipping Container Code (SSCC) identifies an individual logistic unit. Two pallets with exactly the same supplement SKU and case count still need distinct identities. Their contents can share a trade item identifier, or GTIN. A customer purchase order can cover both pallets. Neither the item number nor the order number distinguishes one physical pallet from the other. GS1 on identifying a logistic unit · How GTINs identify supplement variants.

If a third-party warehouse builds the pallet, settle allocation with the shipper before anyone orders numbered rolls. Identify the company responsible for allocating SSCCs under the applicable GS1 Company Prefix and the system controlling its numbers. Record whether the physical builder or brand owner takes that role in your arrangement. A printer can reproduce authorized label data. Choosing a printer does not assign responsibility for the identifiers. GS1 Logistic Label Guideline, sections 3 and 9.

Compare the two workflows at the point of use

Preprinted SSCC-only stock Printing when the pallet is built
Receive a controlled set of allocated IDs. Keep unused, issued and spoiled stock distinguishable. Use the warehouse record for the finished pallet and obtain its authorized ID through the agreed allocation system.
Apply the label to one pallet. Scan it there and associate the ID with the items and quantities actually packed. Print the ID and required warehouse data for that pallet. Apply the label there and scan back what was applied.
Choose this route if the receiver accepts an SSCC-only physical label and can obtain the other required data through the agreed record. Choose this route if changing label fields and printer access favor printing at the build station. Agree what the crew does during a printer outage.

Ask the receiver for its routing guide. Record which data it wants printed, which it wants electronically, the expected label positions and the timing of any advance ship notice (ASN). The GS1 guideline describes how the identifier links a physical unit to data. A particular retailer’s layout, message and receiving appointment are requirements to document with that partner. If it asks for duplicate labels on two faces of the same intact unit, both copies carry the same SSCC.

Follow a test pallet from build to loading

Have the warehouse build a test unit with a known contents list. On a mixed pallet, keep both product lines and their actual counts in the contents record. Scan the label on the completed unit, open the record that scan retrieves and check the lines against the pallet. At loading, compare the scanned ID with the staged shipment. Have the receiving partner check its test message if electronic notice is part of the agreement.

Keep a single example showing the physical pallet reference, SSCC, contents record, shipment reference and the receiving partner’s result. Use the partner’s test environment or agreed procedure. If scanning the pallet returns the record for its neighbor, hold both units and reconcile the physical labels before releasing the shipment. A successful barcode read cannot tell the picker which pallet the label was meant for.

Handle a torn label differently from a rebuilt pallet

  • Damaged label on an unchanged unit. Retrieve the assigned SSCC from the verified record. Authorize a replacement with that same ID and control the damaged copy so it cannot identify another pallet.
  • Spoiled unused preprint. Record its serial in the stock control system and withdraw it from available stock. An unaccounted-for preprint can later be applied to the wrong load.
  • Split or rebuilt unit. Close the old physical unit’s record, remove obsolete labels and assign a distinct SSCC to each newly formed unit. Reconcile the new contents and revise the shipment or ASN with the receiver before loading.

When requesting print pricing, distinguish authorized preprinted serial stock from blank stock for a warehouse printer. Include the receiver’s guide, allocation owner, print and scan locations, surface, print method, roll dimensions and volume by format. Ask RP Labels which stock and print scope it can quote. If retail pack labels are also in the order, specify them separately from the pallet labels. Custom supplement labels for the retail pack · Discuss your pallet label print requirements.
