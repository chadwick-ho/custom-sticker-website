How to Choose

Start from the Pack and Buying Decision

Tell us what will be labelled, roughly how many designs and pieces per design you need, and whether copy and artwork are ready. We can discuss a practical print construction after the application is clear.

  • Choose supplement labels for bottle and jar products, facts-panel space, carton sets and flavor or count variants.
  • Choose peptide vial labels for small research containers, batch/lot fields and application conditions.
  • Choose 3D holographic labels when you need a defined seal surface, visual effect or printed QR area; digital verification is a separate workflow.
