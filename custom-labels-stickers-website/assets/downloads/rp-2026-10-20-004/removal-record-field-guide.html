Removal Record: use notes

These rows are proposed sample requests, not completed tests. The package owner identifies the actual supplier item and surface; the material supplier supplies the proposed construction and relevant application/service guidance. Application and receiving owners agree preparation, conditions, timing, opening action and the acceptance rule before observing specimens. Record actual conditions rather than inventing a dwell time. The observer completes response, photographs and deviations; the buyer release owner records the decision. Differing conditions limit comparisons. A flat-panel result is not automatically a closure result. Do not treat VOID transfer, fragmentation or damage to the pack as an automatic pass.

Field responsibilities

Identify package and candidate construction; agree conditions and action; record observed response and release separately.

Examples are hypothetical planning inputs. Blank observations and approvals are intentionally unknown, not zero or passed. The buyer records an evidence reference and the authorized decision only after the work occurs.
