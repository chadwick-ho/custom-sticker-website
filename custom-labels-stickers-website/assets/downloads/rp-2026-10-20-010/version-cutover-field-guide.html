Version Cutover — use notes

This is a planning handoff, not authorization to use or destroy stock. Buyer owners identify the SKU, exact old/new artwork revisions, roll/carton identities and cutover condition. Inventory owner records the authorized old-stock disposition. Packing owner records actual work-area clearance and first applied new reference. Release owner signs only after the required checks. Where old use is permitted, the eligible run and stop condition must be explicit; where a hold applies, segregate and identify stock until disposition is approved. Review changed code data, dieline and construction through their relevant evidence routes.

Resolve open points

Identify old/new stock, agree cutover and disposition, clear the work area and release the first applied new reference.

Keep proposed evidence separate from observed results and owner approval. Missing data is unknown, not passed. Examples are fictional planning inputs, not orders, tests or project authorization.
