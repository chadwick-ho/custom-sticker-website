Using the color-legend record

Fictional planning example only. No customer, test result, supplier acceptance or approval is provided.

  1. Define the private brand attribute the color signals. Do not import medical, specimen or clinical color meanings.
  2. Retain readable product text or codes independently of color. Record the effective artwork and semantic owner.
  3. Review any repeated cue with a different proposed meaning. Resolve the grouping or text distinction before release.
  4. Approve physical color references separately; keep former legend versions for stock changeover.
