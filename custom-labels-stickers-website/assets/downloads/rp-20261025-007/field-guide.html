Using the conformability-review record

Fictional planning example. No production measurement, supplier promise or approval is supplied.

  1. Keep the unaltered example separate. Replace DEMO identities with the actual vial, artwork and sample references.
  2. Identify the owner and source for every proposed input. Identify every changed construction component. A loose sample does not establish roll dispensing; a changed finished stack does not isolate the effect of one component.
  3. Agree the actual sample scope, observation method and criteria with the buyer, converter and application owner. Leave unavailable observations blank.
  4. Record the actual evidence, responsible decision and remaining hold. PENDING is not a pass and the example is not an acceptance standard.
