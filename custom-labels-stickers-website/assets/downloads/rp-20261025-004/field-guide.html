How to use the copy-panel record

Planning example only. No observation, supplier promise or approval has been obtained.

  1. Replace DEMO references with actual bottle, order and artwork identities. Retain the unaltered example separately.
  2. Identify the accepted specification and owner for each unresolved field. A requested specification is not supplier acceptance.
  3. Record source documents and the actual observations needed for this decision. Do not convert blank fields into zero or a pass.
  4. Have the responsible buyer, supplier or line owner supply the missing decision within their actual scope. Record the exact released reference and remaining holds.
