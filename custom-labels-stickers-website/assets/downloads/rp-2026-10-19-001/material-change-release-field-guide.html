Material Change Release — filling guide

The supplier identifies both complete constructions and changed components. The buyer links retained specification/sample references and marks unknowns. For each changed function, the packaging or line owner agrees the necessary evidence, actual container, application/use conditions, observation times and acceptance criteria before recording a result. The example has no results or approval; never copy those fields as passed. Review whether existing qualification really applies. The approval owner identifies affected order/SKUs/quantity and chooses a one-order exception or a standing approved alternative, then obtains supplier acknowledgement. Record the actual construction on receiving records so returning to the original is deliberate. A new lot of the same approved construction follows agreed incoming checks, not an automatically invented full requalification.
