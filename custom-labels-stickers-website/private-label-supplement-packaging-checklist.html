The short answer: treat the bottle label, carton and seal as one version-controlled project. Decide what belongs on each surface, lock the approved text before prepress, and proof every SKU at actual size. Printing can reproduce approved content; it cannot decide whether product claims or regulatory statements are lawful in your market.

Start with a packaging map

List every printed component: bottle label, lid or neck seal, carton, insert and shipping identifier. Give each component a file name, finished size, material, quantity and version owner. This simple map prevents the common mistake of approving the bottle artwork while an older carton file remains in production.

Bottle labelPrimary product identity, approved information panel, barcode or QR area and batch or date field.
CartonMore room for brand story, handling information and a clear hierarchy, subject to the buyer's approved copy.
Tamper sealA small functional element that must bridge the actual opening and adhere to both package surfaces.
Master recordSKU name, revision date, color cue, barcode file, quantities and approval status for every component.

Separate regulatory approval from print approval

The United States FDA identifies required categories of information for dietary supplement labels, including a statement of identity, net quantity, nutrition labeling, ingredient information and the responsible business name and address. Requirements vary by product and destination, so use the FDA Dietary Supplement Labeling Guide as a starting reference for US projects and obtain qualified review for your exact product and market.

A printer's proof confirms dimensions, image placement, color layers, finishing notes and production setup. It is not legal approval. Mark the copy file as approved before sending it to prepress, and keep regulatory comments out of scattered chat messages that can be missed during revision.

Build one hierarchy across bottle and box

Product name, variant, count and brand should be recognized the same way on every component. The carton can carry more detail, but it should not look like a different SKU. Use a stable product-code system and a controlled accent color rather than redesigning the hierarchy for each flavor, ingredient or strength.

Send codes as production assets, not screenshots

Provide the correct barcode file and the final destination for each QR code. Do not rebuild a code from a screenshot or low-resolution preview. Keep a quiet zone around scannable elements, avoid placing them on a tight curve or reflective effect, and test them from a printed sample after the label is applied to the real bottle.

Make variant control visible

A SKU matrix should show product name, artwork filename, color cue, barcode, label quantity and carton quantity. The printed proof should display the SKU name and revision date outside the artwork area. Approve the whole family together so a copied barcode, outdated ingredient panel or mismatched box can be caught before production.

Files and approvals checklist

  • Actual container and carton dimensions, dielines and clear photos
  • Final approved copy in an editable document
  • Vector logo, linked images and brand color references
  • Original barcode files and tested QR destinations
  • SKU matrix with artwork names, quantities and revision dates
  • Material, finish and application method for each component
  • Named regulatory approver and named print approver
  • Actual-size digital proof followed by a physical sample when risk is high

FAQ

What files should I send for private label supplement packaging?

Send approved copy, brand assets, dimensions, dielines, barcode files, variant list, quantities and market information. Vector artwork or a print-ready PDF is preferred.

Can a label printer approve supplement claims or regulatory copy?

No. A printer can flag production concerns, but the buyer remains responsible for qualified regulatory review and final approved copy.

Should bottle labels and cartons use one master artwork system?

Yes. Shared hierarchy, SKU codes, color logic and version records reduce mismatches while allowing each component to serve a different function.
