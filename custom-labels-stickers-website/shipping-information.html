Shipping Information

We support worldwide shipping for custom labels and stickers. Shipping method and cost are confirmed before order based on destination, quantity and delivery timeline.

Worldwide Shipping

Custom label and sticker orders can be shipped to overseas customers by suitable logistics options. The available method depends on your country, order size, delivery requirement and shipment weight.

Sample Shipping

Samples can be shipped after sample production is completed. Sample freight and expected transit time will be confirmed with your sales contact.

Bulk Order Shipping

Bulk orders are packed after production and inspection. We can discuss express, air or other logistics options according to the order situation.

Tracking and Cost Confirmation

Tracking information will be provided when available. Shipping cost should be confirmed before the order is finalized because it may vary by country, quantity and shipping method.
