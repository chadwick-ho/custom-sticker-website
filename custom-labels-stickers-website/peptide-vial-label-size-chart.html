Peptide Vial Label Size Chart for 2ml, 3ml, 5ml and 10ml Bottles

The right peptide vial label size is the size that stays flat on the bottle, keeps the main text readable and leaves enough room for batch, code or QR information. Start with the bottle diameter, not the artwork file.

Buyers often ask for a "standard peptide vial label size", but small glass vials are not as standard as they look. Two bottles can both be called 3ml and still have different shoulder curves, diameters and usable label areas. That is why a practical label size chart should be treated as a starting point, then confirmed with a bottle photo or sample.

Quick size planning chart

Use this chart to estimate the label direction before artwork. The final size should be checked against the real bottle, especially if the bottle has a short straight wall or a strong shoulder curve.

2ml vialUse a compact front label. Keep only the logo, product name or code, and one small tracking field. Avoid long copy.
3ml vialGood for a clean front label with product name, strength or code field, batch field and a small color band.
5ml vialAllows a larger brand block and clearer spacing. A small QR code is more realistic here than on a very narrow vial.
10ml vialCan carry a more complete label system, including product name, code, batch, QR, color coding and a small warning or handling note if needed.

How to measure the usable label area

Measure the straight wall of the bottle first. The label should not climb too far into the shoulder curve, because the edge can lift and the design may wrinkle. Then measure the bottle circumference. The label width should leave a small gap unless you intentionally want a full-wrap design.

If you do not have exact measurements, send a clear front photo, side photo and the bottle capacity. RP can suggest a practical layout and confirm it in the free digital proof.

Send these details for an accurate proof

  • Bottle capacity: 2ml, 3ml, 5ml, 10ml or another size
  • One straight front photo and one side photo of the bottle
  • Cap color and bottle color, clear or amber
  • Required text fields: product name, code, batch, QR, barcode or logo
  • Application format: roll labels, sheet labels or labels plus box packaging
  • Material preference: white film, clear film, matte film, holographic film or waterproof BOPP/PET

What should be large on a small vial label?

Do not make every line important. On a small vial, the product name or code should win the layout. The secondary fields should be smaller but still readable. If you need multiple versions, use color bands or series codes so packers can separate them quickly.

For QR codes, leave quiet space around the code. If the code is too small or printed over a busy holographic background, scanning can become unreliable. The proof should show the real printed size, not just a zoomed-in artwork preview.

Roll labels or sheet labels?

Roll labels are the better choice for repeat production, faster application and future reorders. Sheet labels can be useful for small trial runs, samples or when you are still testing several designs. If you plan to scale the product line, roll direction and label gap should be confirmed before bulk production.

Material also changes the size decision

Clear film can make the bottle look cleaner, but it needs strong contrast in the text. White BOPP or PET is easier for small type and batch fields. Holographic film adds shelf impact and brand protection, but the layout should avoid thin gray text or low-contrast details.

Practical recommendation

If you are unsure, do not guess the final size from another seller's bottle. Send the bottle photo, label content and target quantity first. RP can prepare a free digital proof from 100 pcs, then adjust the label size before sample or bulk production.

FAQ

Can one label size fit 3ml and 5ml vials?

Sometimes, but it depends on the bottle diameter and usable straight wall. A label that looks good on a 5ml bottle may wrap too far on a 3ml bottle.

Should I choose a full-wrap label?

A full-wrap label can look premium, but it leaves less tolerance during application. For small bottles, many buyers prefer a front label with a clean side gap.

Can I order only 100 pcs?

Many RP custom vial label projects start from 100 pcs, depending on material, size and finish.
