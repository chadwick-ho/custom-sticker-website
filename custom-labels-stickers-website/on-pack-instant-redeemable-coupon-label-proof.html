How to Proof an Instant Redeemable Coupon Label

Release an on-pack coupon order once the retailer has checked the detached coupon with the eligible product and your packaging reviewer has approved the bottle both before and after removal. A clean peel answers only part of the buying question. A shopper could remove the offer before another shopper picks up the bottle, or a cashier could accept the bottle while the coupon fails under the retailer’s rules. Keep a proof of each state.

Lock the offer to a retailer, SKU and time window

Ask the promotion owner for the approved offer wording, eligible sizes or counts, participating retailers, start and end dates, and any purchase conditions. Put the offer version next to the artwork revision. Name the party that provides the coupon data and the retailer contact who can arrange a checkout test. If a retailer changes the dates, issue a new proof for that retailer before releasing the affected stock.

Imagine that two counts of the same supplement share a bottle shape and only the larger count is eligible. A beautiful coupon placed on the smaller bottle still advertises the wrong offer. Give the packing crew an application list keyed to the eligible product identifiers. Keep the item identifier on the bottle distinguishable from any identifier supplied for redemption.

Examine what comes away and what the shopper keeps

Some on-pack coupons use a base that stays on the package while the shopper takes the upper piece to the register. Specify which construction is being priced and ask for a sample on the intended bottle and finish. A hinged information panel has a different job. Labels & Specialty Products on coupon constructions · Proofing a peel-and-reveal information label.

  • Intact bottle. Photograph the pack at the planned placement. Check that the product identity, approved bottle information and retail code remain accessible. Have someone locate the tab with the bottle in hand. Check that neighboring bottles in the pack do not catch it during handling.
  • Detached coupon. Peel it as a shopper or cashier would. Check whether the tear cut through an offer condition or identifier, and whether exposed adhesive makes the piece hard to handle. Read the offer from the piece the retailer will actually receive.
  • Bottle after removal. Inspect the base and surrounding print for lifted ink, adhesive or a covered instruction. An exposed patch that still appears to offer a discount can mislead the next shopper. Take a second bottle photo from the original angle and approve the remaining pack on its own.

Ask the retailer to run the checkout proof

Give the retailer the eligible bottle and its detached coupon. A converter describes this handoff as presenting the removed offer at the register. The retailer decides how its own checkout recognizes the item and processes the offer. Ask its tester to follow the normal sequence and record the expected discount, the result shown and the offer revision. A readable retail code establishes the product identity. The offer rules still determine whether a discount applies. Symbology’s checkout coupon example · Checking identifiers at a retail point of sale.

With the retailer’s permission, include a neighboring ineligible SKU or a test offer outside its date window. An expected refusal is a useful result when the register applies it to the correct combination. Record the retailer, date, eligible item, test case and reviewer. If the coupon separates neatly but the retailer sees the wrong offer value, send the case back to the promotion owner before ordering print.

Close out the offer and its leftover stock

Before ordering, assign someone to count unused coupons and already-labeled bottles by retailer and offer revision. Agree on a last application date that allows for packing and distribution. At campaign close, hold leftover rolls for the promotion owner’s disposition and identify affected finished bottles for the retailer’s instructions. Carry a coupon into a later campaign only after the owner approves its wording, dates and data for that campaign.

For an inquiry, send the approved offer, retailer specification, data owner, eligible product list, dates, bottle artwork and photos of the proposed tab area. Include the application method and quantity for each offer version. Ask which coupon construction and proof quantities RP Labels can quote. For the underlying bottle, use the product page to organize the container and approved copy. Custom supplement labels · Ask about an on-pack coupon proof.
