Measure Before Choosing Artwork

Nominal capacity such as 2 ml or 10 ml does not define a bottle's outside diameter. Measure the actual container and keep the label inside the usable straight wall. For tapered jars, measure the usable label wall at the height where the label will sit; do not use the widest or narrowest diameter alone. For squeeze tubes, measure the usable flat or slightly curved panel in the application area and allow for the tube changing shape when filled or squeezed. A physical fit check is recommended before mass production.

A gap exposes a strip of bottle. An overlap places one label edge over the printed face, so adhesion to that finish must be checked. If an overlap is chosen, a small controlled overlap is set during layout and must be confirmed on the actual container. This site does not treat any single overlap width as a universal standard. See the gap and overlap guide.

What Changes a Custom Label Quote?

Custom label unit price depends on material, size, finishing, quantity, and the number of designs. A total delivered price, including freight to a stated destination, is given on the written quote. This page does not publish a price list. Multiple designs in the same size can be quoted and produced, including orders such as 500 pcs each for several designs. When the process allows, jobs may share setup or be combined. Any saving is stated on the written quote and is not a fixed percentage. Custom die-cut shapes and certain print or foil processes may require tooling or plate/die charges. Whether a charge applies, how it is billed, and whether it can be reused on a reorder is confirmed on the written quote. See plate and die charges. Send the quantity per design, intended storage conditions and application method. For machine rolls, include core size, maximum outside diameter, unwind direction and label gap.

When is the calculation useful?

For a straight, circular bottle with a wrap label. It helps prepare a paper fit test and a specification for your supplier. It does not account for bottle manufacturing variation, adhesive performance, taper, a cap bridge or print bleed.

What does a proof confirm?

Before mass production we prepare a digital proof that shows layout, size, and appearance. A 3D bottle mockup, if requested, is a visual reference only and is not a color or dimensional standard. Whether a proof is complimentary is stated on the written quote. A physical sample checks fit, finish and application. Sample lead time and cost are confirmed before the sample starts. For refrigeration, oils or tamper evidence, define an exposure or removal test on the actual package. See the sample policy.

What should accompany an inquiry?

A bottle photo or drawing, label dimensions, quantity per design, artwork status, material preference, destination and deadline. Send print files in your email or WhatsApp conversation after preparing the brief.
