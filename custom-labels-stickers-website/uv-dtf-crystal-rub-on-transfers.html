UV DTF Crystal Transfers for Glass Cups

We can produce UV DTF crystal (rub-on) transfers for glass cups and similar hard surfaces, including designs with no visible background film after application. Application surface, curing, and handling instructions are confirmed with the order. This page does not publish a wash, dishwasher, or outdoor durability rating unless a written test record supports it.

No visible background film

The transfer can be produced so the design sits on the glass without a visible background film after application. That description is about the finished look of the graphic. It is not a wash rating, a dishwasher rating, or an outdoor durability rating.

This site does not have a written test record that would support those ratings, so none is published here. If you need a durability check, say so on the order and we will confirm what can actually be reviewed. Do not treat a product photo as that record.

This is not UV ink or spot UV on a label

UV-cured label printing and a spot UV coating are a different question. That comparison is on UV-printed labels versus spot UV. A glass-cup rub-on transfer is specified on its own quote.

What the order needs to name

Send the cup or other hard surface, the artwork, the quantity, and how the transfer will be applied. Application surface, curing, and handling instructions are confirmed with the order. The delivered price is on the written quote. This page does not publish a price list. Prepare the brief on the quote planner.

Digital printing for many custom label jobs can start at 100 pcs. Foil stamping, matte black materials, and other special finishes may have a different minimum, confirmed on the written quote. The quantity on the written quote is the order minimum for that job. See the FAQ.
