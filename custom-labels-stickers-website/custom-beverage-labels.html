Moisture Resistance for Cold Drink Use

Beverage labels often face condensation, cold storage, handling and shelf display needs. Waterproof BOPP, PET and moisture-resistant film materials can help keep labels stable and clean-looking in suitable beverage packaging applications.

Roll labels are available for manual labeling or automatic labeling machines. We can discuss roll direction, label gap and application needs before production.
