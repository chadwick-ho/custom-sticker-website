Most sticker buyers do not need a long technical lecture. They need to know whether the artwork can be printed cleanly, whether the edge shape will look right, whether the material matches the selling scene, and whether the final pack is easy to ship or resell.

Start with the final selling format

A loose die cut sticker, a kiss cut sheet, a sticker roll and a retail sticker pack are different products. Before asking for a quote, decide how the customer will receive the sticker: single pieces, sheets, small packs, roll format or backing-card packs.

Information to send for a fast quote

  • Sticker size or sheet size
  • Quantity, starting from 100 pcs
  • Die cut, kiss cut, sticker sheet or roll format
  • Material choice: vinyl, waterproof vinyl, paper, clear film or holographic film
  • Finish: matte, glossy, holographic, transparent or laminated
  • Artwork file, logo, reference image or simple idea
  • Packing method: loose, bagged, sheet set or retail pack

Die cut, kiss cut or sticker sheet?

Die cut stickers are cut to the outer shape and work well for brand stickers, gift stickers and promotional giveaways. Kiss cut stickers stay on a backing sheet, which makes peeling easier and keeps small designs organized. Sticker sheets are useful when a buyer wants several designs sold together as one item.

Keep artwork rights and print quality clean

For commercial production, use original artwork or artwork you have the right to print. If the file is not ready, send the best version you have. RP can help check resolution, edge spacing, cut-line position and proof layout before production.

Choose material based on the use

Waterproof vinyl is a good default for bottles, laptops, outdoor use and promotional stickers. Matte paper is softer and cost-effective for dry packaging. Holographic material adds shelf impact for creative brands, sticker shops and limited collections.

Simple custom path

Send artwork or an idea, confirm the sticker size and format, check the free digital proof, then approve sample and bulk production. MOQ starts from 100 pcs. Sample lead time is usually 1-2 days, and bulk production is usually 10-12 days after confirmation.

Common buyer questions

Can you help if my artwork is not print-ready?

Yes. Send your current file or reference image. RP can help prepare a free design suggestion and proof for checking before production.

Can you make sticker sheets with multiple designs?

Yes. Send the sheet size, design count and preferred spacing. We can arrange the layout for proof confirmation.

What material should I choose for waterproof stickers?

Waterproof vinyl or laminated film materials are usually the best choice for stickers that may be touched, carried, shipped or exposed to moisture.
