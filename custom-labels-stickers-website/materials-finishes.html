Materials & Finishes

Different labels need different materials. We help you choose suitable options based on bottle type, usage environment, finish preference, labeling method and budget.

Ask Us to Recommend a Material

Custom Label Materials

These materials are commonly used for custom labels, bottle labels, sticker sheets and packaging stickers.

Waterproof BOPP

A durable film material for bottle labels, beverage labels and product labels that need moisture resistance.

Vinyl

Flexible and durable, suitable for die cut stickers, cartoon stickers, brand stickers and promotional applications.

Clear Label Material

Creates a transparent label effect for bottles, jars and packaging where a clean no-label look is preferred.

PET

Strong, smooth and premium-looking, suitable for labels that need better durability and shape stability.

Matte Paper

A clean and natural option for packaging stickers, gift labels and products that need a softer surface feel.

Glossy Paper

A bright and cost-effective option for promotional stickers, brand labels and general packaging use.

Holographic Material

Adds a colorful reflective effect for cartoon stickers, character stickers, gift stickers and creative packaging.

Finishing and Production Formats

Matte Finish

Soft, modern and less reflective for premium packaging.

Glossy Finish

Bright and shiny, helping colors look more vivid.

Foil Stamping

Adds metallic details for logos, borders or premium effects.

Die Cut

Cuts stickers or labels into custom outer shapes.

Kiss Cut

Cuts the sticker layer while keeping the backing sheet.

Transparent Label

Uses clear material for a clean bottle appearance.

Roll Labels

Suitable for manual application or labeling machines.

Sticker Sheets

Multiple designs arranged on one sheet for retail and gifts.

Recommended Materials by Product

Cartoon Stickers

Vinyl, waterproof vinyl, glossy paper, matte paper, holographic material and clear material for sticker sheets and die cut stickers.

Beverage Labels

Waterproof BOPP, PET, clear label material, oil-resistant material and moisture-resistant material for bottles and cans.
