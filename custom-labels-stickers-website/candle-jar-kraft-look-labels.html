Candle jars

Candle Jar Labels, Including a Kraft-Paper Look

We can produce labels for candle jars, including a kraft-paper look. Uncoated kraft paper is not waterproof. If the jar will see moisture, wax, oil, or condensation, we specify a laminated kraft-look paper or a film/synthetic material instead of untreated kraft. Suitability is confirmed for your jar and use.

Uncoated kraft is not a waterproof label

A kraft color can be the look you want on a candle jar. Untreated kraft paper does not become waterproof because the product is a candle. Water, wax, oil, and condensation are reasons to leave untreated kraft and choose a protected construction.

The two routes we specify for that exposure are a laminated kraft-look paper, or a film/synthetic material. The quote names which one is proposed for your jar. A material name on this site is not a certified construction list.

Confirm the jar, not a catalog photo

Send the jar, or its measurements and a photo of the real container, plus the conditions the label will meet: moisture, wax, oil, condensation, or ordinary dry handling. Also send the label size, quantity per design, and artwork status. Pricing is on the quote planner path: the delivered total is on the written quote, and this page does not publish a price list.

Digital printing for many custom label jobs can start at 100 pcs. Foil, matte black, and other special finishes may have a different minimum. The quantity on the written quote is the order minimum for that job. See the FAQ.
