# RP Labels: primary buyer positioning test

Date: 2026-10-17. This is a reversible positioning hypothesis, not a validated customer-conversion finding.

## Evidence and boundary

- First-party source: GSC export `rplabels.com-Performance-on-Search-2026-10-07.xlsx` in the user's Downloads folder. In its three-month window, `supplement label printing` had 39 impressions, `custom supplement labels` 37, and the supplement category page 163 impressions, all with 0 clicks in the exported rows. The peptide vial size chart had 670 impressions and 11 clicks, but size-chart traffic is not necessarily procurement traffic.
- Existing website capability: custom printed supplement bottle, jar, carton and roll labels; peptide-vial and holographic-label categories; contact brief with quantity per design and artwork stage. Delivery claims on the site still need current operational verification before being used as competitive proof.
- Public question examples and limitations are recorded in [precision-buyer-brief.md](precision-buyer-brief.md). No qualified-inquiry or win/loss dataset was available for this positioning decision. Search visibility is not proof of payment intent.

## Positioning sentence

RP Labels helps small supplement brands turn an actual bottle or jar and approved product copy into custom printed labels, with the size, material, application method and quantity for each design specified before proof. The site also supports specialist research-vial and holographic-seal projects. RP prints labels; the buyer approves product claims and regulatory text.

Do not interpret "small" as a fixed headcount or annual revenue threshold. The observable fit is a real packaging project with a container/surface, design count, per-design quantity, artwork/copy stage and a route to purchasing approval.

The homepage is a supplier/brand introduction and should not duplicate the full product query intent of a category page. Reuse or improve these URLs before creating a near-synonym article.

## Lead quality and review

- A potentially qualified inquiry names a real packaging use, a container or application surface, an approximate design count and quantity per design, and a reachable buyer or coordinator. Artwork and budget may still be pending; do not mark them unqualified solely for that reason.
- A purely informational size-chart visit or a request for a QR authentication platform is not automatically a label purchase lead.
- Review GSC page/query trends and actual inquiry briefs weekly. Record product type, first run/reorder, container, designs, per-design quantity, copy/artwork status, stage and buyer objection. Compare the three category paths by qualified projects and progress to proof/quote, not pageviews alone.
- If supplement inquiries prove less qualified than a specialist path, revise the homepage emphasis. Do not infer ranking or conversion improvement from this content change alone.
